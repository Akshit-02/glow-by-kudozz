# Dot & Key — Excluded Products

**84 products excluded** from individual-blog eligibility.

## Exclusion Methodology

Dot & Key's own Shopify catalogue tags every product with an official `product_type` field. This crawl used that official first-party classification (not keyword guessing) as the primary exclusion signal, cross-checked against product titles and, for ambiguous cases, full ingredient/SKU comparison:

- **`product_type: Combo`** — bundles, duos, trios, kits, and "pack of 2" multipacks that repackage two or more already-catalogued individual products into one SKU.
- **`product_type: Free`** — promotional gift-with-purchase minis and trial sizes that share the exact same SKU as a full-size standalone product already counted once, or are not sold as a primary purchasable item on their own.
- **SKU-duplicate marketing pages** — a small number of URLs (tagged `not-searchable` / `comparison-product` internally by Dot & Key) point to the *exact same SKU* as an already-listed standalone product. These are alternate landing pages for the same product (e.g., used in on-site comparison tables), not separate products, so only the canonical/searchable URL is counted once.
